Why free

Why is the SEO audit free?

The audit is free because I can't honestly price work on a site I haven't seen from the inside. Without it, we would both be guessing.

One business needs two days of technical fixes. The next needs a new structure and twenty pages of copy. A third doesn't need SEO right now at all, just better photos and a clearer offer. The audit tells those three apart.

If you have a site and are considering any service from me, the audit comes first. It is never added to an invoice.

How it works

Three steps, and no twenty-field form

  1. You send me the address. By phone, email or the form, whichever suits you. Read access to Google Analytics and Search Console helps. If you don't have them, that is itself a finding.
  2. I go through the site and the competition. It takes two to four working days, depending on size and number of languages. You get a list of findings, each with a priority and an effort estimate.
  3. We talk for thirty minutes. We go down the list: what to fix this week, what this quarter and what not to do at all. Only then does talking about price make sense.
Honestly

What this audit is not

Not a 100-page PDF. Tool-generated reports look impressive and say nothing about order. For a small business, the order is the whole job. You get a list you can read over a coffee.

Not the fix. The audit says what to do. Doing it is separate work at a separate price. Some findings you can fix yourself in an hour, and I'll tell you which ones.

Not a promise of first place. Nobody can guarantee a top ranking or a mention in ChatGPT. If SEO isn't your best spend right now, I'll say so, even if it costs me the job.

FAQ

SEO audit questions

What does an SEO audit cost?

Nothing, if you already have a site and are considering any service from me. You get a list of findings and a 30-minute call. If you then order nothing, you keep the list and owe me nothing.

What exactly do you check?

Six layers: technical health, keywords against competitors, content and page structure, the Google Business Profile, AI search visibility and analytics. Every finding carries a priority and an estimate of the work involved.

How long does it take?

Two to four working days, depending on the size of the site and the number of languages. I only need the address, plus read access to Google Analytics and Search Console if you have them.

Is the audit the same as an SEO strategy?

No. The audit says what is wrong and what to do first. The €300 SEO strategy is a keyword map assigned to pages, competitor research and a content plan for several months.

What if I don't have a website yet?

Then there is nothing to audit. Instead, I look at the businesses ranking above you and show what they rely on, before we design anything. That work is part of the website package.
