    FAQ

    Pricing questions

    How much does a small business website cost?

    €500 for up to 4 pages and €800 for up to 10 pages, net and paid once. Sites with more than 10 pages start at €800. SEO strategy, keyword research, Search Console and GA4 are included in every build.

    Are the prices net or gross?

    Net. Invoices are issued by imarket2web, NIP 9721328238, Poland, and VAT is added.

    Is the calculator result a binding quote?

    No. It is a ballpark range based on your answers. The final figure is confirmed after a short call about scope, and it is written down before any work starts.

    Do I need a monthly plan?

    No. Most projects are one-off: a website or a specific service. The monthly growth plan only makes sense if you want content, links and ads to keep working every month.

    Why does each extra language cost 35% more?

    Because each language gets its own keyword research and its own copy. People search differently in each language, so a translated page rarely ranks as well as one written for that market.
