04 — How the work gets made

AI does the volume. I do the judgement.

Accelerated by AI
  • Keyword, SERP and competitor research at scale
  • Clustering intent into a page architecture
  • First drafts, outlines and content repurposing
  • Review reply drafts and FAQ answers
  • Scheduling, routing, reporting, notifications
Stays human
  • Strategy, and what we choose not to do
  • Brand voice and every published word
  • Anything a customer reads with your name on it
  • Design decisions and quality control
  • Reading the numbers and deciding the next move

Every automation that touches a customer has an approval step in front of it. Nothing goes out under your brand that a human has not read.
