Local SEO for a one-person studio
Local SEO · 9 min read

Most local SEO advice is written for businesses with a marketing department. This is the version for a studio of one: what moves the map pack, what wastes an afternoon, and the order to do it in with two hours a week.

Google Business ProfileReviewsMap pack
What these notes are

Written after the work, not instead of it.

Everything here comes out of a real project for a real small business: a permanent makeup studio, a massage practice, a tattoo studio, a game art company. When a number is quoted, it is a number I watched move. When something is called a waste of money, it is usually because I spent it.

That is the whole selection rule. There is no publishing schedule and no attempt to cover the subject, because notes written to fill a calendar are worth what they cost to write. A post appears when a job teaches something a different owner could use.

What gets written about
  • Local SEO: the map pack, Google Business Profiles, reviews
  • What to build first when the budget only covers one thing
  • Being found by ChatGPT and other assistants, not only Google
  • Costs, in actual numbers, and what they bought
  • The mistakes, including the ones that were mine

Who it is for

For the owner who is also the marketing department. Almost all local SEO advice quietly assumes a team, a budget and someone whose job this is. If you have two hours a week and you are also the person doing the treatments, most of that advice is not wrong so much as impossible, and the order it tells you to work in is the wrong order for you.

So the notes are written for two hours a week. They say what to do first, what can wait a quarter, and what to skip entirely. If something here would help but you would rather not do it yourself, the services page covers the same ground as work, and a free audit will tell you which part of it you actually need.
