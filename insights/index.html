Where they come from

What are these guides based on?

Every guide comes out of a real project for a real small business. When a number is quoted, it is one I watched move. When something is called a waste of money, it is usually because I spent it.

The projects behind them include a permanent makeup studio, a massage practice, a tattoo studio and a game art company. You can read about each one in the SEO case studies.

There is no publishing schedule. Guides written to fill a calendar are worth what they cost to write.

Topics

What topics do the guides cover?

The guides cover local SEO for small service businesses, what to build first on a small budget, and visibility in AI assistants.

  • Local SEO: the map pack, Google Business Profiles and reviews
  • What to build first when the budget covers only one thing
  • Being found by ChatGPT and other AI assistants, not only by Google
  • Costs, in real numbers, and what they bought
  • Mistakes, including my own
Who it is for

Who are these guides for?

They are for the owner who is also the marketing department. Most local SEO advice assumes a team and a budget. With two hours a week, that advice is not wrong so much as impossible.

So each guide says what to do first, what can wait a quarter and what to skip. If you would rather not do it yourself, the local SEO service covers the same ground as paid work. A free SEO audit tells you which part you actually need.
