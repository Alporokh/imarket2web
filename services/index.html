Core services

Four core services, each with its own page

01 - Local

Local SEO

Local SEO is the work that decides whether you appear in the Google map pack when someone nearby searches for your service. I set up the profile, the pages and the review routine that together earn that position.

Best for: a studio or clinic serving one city.
Price: part of the €300 SEO strategy, or included in a website package.

02 - AI search

AI SEO & GEO

AI SEO is the work of making your business easy for ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to understand and describe. It builds on classic SEO with clearer facts, structured data and answer-first pages.

Best for: businesses whose customers already ask AI assistants for recommendations.
Price: part of the €300 SEO strategy, or quoted after the free audit.

AI SEO and GEO explained

Google Business Profile optimization

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a customer sees, before your website. I fix categories, services, description, photos and the review routine.

Add-ons

Supporting services

These services add to the four above. Each one can be bought separately, and each one is priced in the table at the top of this page.

01 - Search

SEO strategy & technical SEO

SEO strategy is a keyword-to-page map, competitor research and a content plan, delivered as one document for €300.

Most audits hand you a 60-page PDF. You get the three things that are actually costing you positions, in the order I would fix them.

What you get
  • Technical audit: crawl, indexing, speed and structured data
  • A keyword-to-page map, so no two pages compete for the same search
  • On-page work across titles, headings and internal links
  • Search Console set up and read, not just installed
02 - Content

SEO content & strategy

Content gets written when it earns visibility, authority, a conversion or a better-informed customer.

The questions people send you in private messages are the brief.

What you get
  • Service pages that answer the objection, not just describe the service
  • FAQ sections that also cover long-tail search
  • An editorial calendar you can actually sustain
  • Copy in any language you sell in, researched as its own market; English, Polish, Ukrainian and Russian come from me first-hand
03 - Automation

AI marketing automation

Marketing automation with n8n takes over the repetitive work: review requests, lead routing, reminders and reporting.

Anything a customer will read passes an approval step first. The first automation worth building is almost always the review request.

What you get
  • Review requests sent at the right point in the customer cycle
  • Enquiries routed to a sheet, an inbox or your phone
  • Draft replies to reviews and FAQs, for you to approve
  • Reports that arrive without you asking
04 - Social

Social media & content automation

A monthly plan where each post supports local visibility, proof or a service you want to sell. If a post does none of those three, it does not get made.

One source piece becomes posts, reels and Google Business updates, published against a calendar you approve in advance.

What you get
  • A 16-post monthly calendar tied to what you sell
  • Google Business Profile posts, which most studios skip entirely
  • Reels assembled from footage you already have
  • An approval gate, so nothing reaches a customer unreviewed
05 - Paid

Google Ads campaigns

Search ads buy the top of the page while SEO earns it. They are worth running when you need enquiries this month, when a service is seasonal, or when a new location has no organic history.

Your ad budget goes to Google, on your own card. What you pay me is the setup and the monthly management, quoted separately. If your budget is too small for management to pay off, I will tell you to spend it on the site instead.

What you get
  • Campaigns built from the same intent research as your pages
  • Conversion tracking for calls, forms and bookings before the first click
  • Negative keyword lists maintained monthly
  • A landing page that matches the ad, not the homepage for everything
06 - Authority

Backlink strategy & digital PR

Links are still how the web decides who is credible, and the ones that work are earned.

I do not buy links: for a local business they rarely move rankings and sometimes cause real damage. I explain why in the local SEO guide for one-person studios.

What you get
  • A link gap audit: where competitors are cited and you are not
  • Relevant sources: associations, suppliers, venues, partners, local press
  • Unlinked mentions of your name turned into links
  • A toxic-link review, and a disavow if a previous agency left damage behind
If you only buy one thing

Which SEO service should you buy first?

Start with the layer that makes the next one cheaper. For most service businesses that is the website, then local SEO, then analytics.

Start with the site

No website, or a bad one

Start with the site. Everything else points at it, and ranking a page that does not convert just brings more people who leave. See web design for service businesses.

Start with local SEO

The site is fine, but nobody finds it

Start with local SEO. For a studio serving one city, the map pack is usually worth more than everything else combined. See the local SEO service.

Start with analytics

You get found, but cannot prove it

Start with analytics. Until you know which page produced the booking, every next decision is a guess with your money behind it.

FAQ

Questions about SEO services

What SEO services does imarket2web offer?

imarket2web offers local SEO, AI SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, web design, SEO strategy, content, marketing automation, social media, Google Ads and backlink strategy. All of it is delivered by one specialist, Alena Porokh, from Poznań.

Can I buy just one service?

Yes. Every service has its own price and can be bought on its own. Many clients start with the €150 Google Business Profile optimization or the €300 SEO strategy.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Google Business Profile changes often show within 2-4 weeks. New pages usually need 2-3 months to rank for local searches, and longer in competitive cities. Reviews and links keep improving positions after that.

Do you work with businesses outside Poland?

Yes. Current clients are in Poznań, Prague and Tallinn. I research each market in its own language, and I write in English, Polish, Ukrainian and Russian first-hand.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. One-off services have no contract beyond the job itself. The monthly plan runs month to month.
