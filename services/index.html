What you get

09 - Paid

Google Ads campaigns

Search ads buy the top of the page while SEO earns it. Worth running when you need enquiries this month, when a service is seasonal, or when a new location has no organic history yet.

Ads are the one layer that stops the day you stop paying. That is not an argument against them - it is an argument for putting them on top of a site that already converts, so you are not renting traffic into a leak.

What you get
  • Campaigns built from the same intent research as your pages, not a fresh keyword dump
  • Conversion tracking before the first click - calls, forms and bookings, so the numbers mean something
  • Negative keyword lists maintained monthly, so you stop paying for the wrong searches
  • Radius, schedule and budget matched to when you can actually answer the phone
  • A landing page that matches the ad, instead of the homepage for everything
  • A monthly review that says what to change, not just what happened
Search campaignsPerformance MaxLocal adsConversion trackingNegative keywords
An honest limit
Your ad spend is not my fee

The budget goes to Google, on your own card, and you can see every euro of it. What you pay me is the build and the monthly management, quoted separately. Below a certain monthly budget the management fee is larger than the result it can buy - if that is where you are, I will tell you to spend the money on the site instead.

10 - Authority

Backlink strategy & digital PR

Links are still how the web decides who is credible. The ones that work are earned, and that difference is the entire strategy.

I will not buy links for you. For a local business they rarely move rankings and occasionally cause real damage - I have written about why. What does move is being worth citing, in the places your market already reads.

What you get
  • A link gap audit - where your competitors are cited and you are not
  • Sources that pass real relevance: associations, suppliers, venues, partners, local press
  • Digital PR angles built from something you actually have - data, a case, a clear opinion
  • Unlinked mentions of your name found and turned into links
  • A toxic-link review, and a disavow if a previous agency left damage behind
