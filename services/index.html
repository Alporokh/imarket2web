01 - Website

Web design & development

Conversion-oriented sites on a static-first stack: fast, crawlable, cheap to host, easy to extend. Strategy and UX before pixels.

The structure comes from what people actually search, not from your internal org chart. If five treatments are searched five different ways, they get five pages - not one price list with five rows.

What you get
  • Page architecture built from real search demand
  • Design system, so later pages cost less than the first
  • Form with automated order routing and Google Sheets
  • Core Web Vitals handled at build time, not patched after
  • Deployed on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare, no monthly platform fee

04 - Content

SEO content & strategy

Content gets written when it earns visibility, authority, a conversion, or a better-informed customer. Not by the article count.

The questions people send you in DMs are the brief. Does it hurt, how long does it last, what happens if it goes wrong - answered on the page, before the form.

What you get
  • Keyword-to-page map before a single word is written
  • Service pages that answer the objection, not just describe
  • FAQ and knowledge sections that also cover long-tail search
  • Editorial calendar you can actually sustain
  • Written in your voice, in any of PL, EN, UA, RU

AI search & GEO

Answer-first structure, clear entities and clean structured data, so an AI assistant can describe your business correctly when someone asks it for a recommendation.

Your customers have already changed how they search. Most agencies are still pretending they have not.
