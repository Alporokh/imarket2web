AI SEO: help ChatGPT and Google's AI describe your business correctly

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is search optimization for answers instead of links. Someone asks an AI assistant: "which massage studio in Poznań is good for back pain?" The assistant writes one answer and names a few businesses. AI SEO makes your business one of the names it can pick, and makes sure the facts it repeats are right.

The field goes by several names. They describe the same work from slightly different angles:

TermShort formWhat it means
AI SEO-The umbrella term: optimizing for AI-generated answers
Generative engine optimizationGEOOptimizing for tools that generate answers, such as ChatGPT and Gemini
Answer engine optimizationAEOStructuring content so any engine, including Google, can lift a direct answer from it

How is AI SEO different from classic SEO?

Classic SEO earns a position in a list of links. AI SEO earns a mention inside a written answer. The foundations are shared, but the finish is different.

AspectClassic SEOAI SEO
GoalRank a page in the top 10 linksBe named and described correctly in an AI answer
What the engine readsPages, links, keywordsFacts about your business from many sources, plus your pages
What winsRelevance and authorityClear, consistent, quotable facts
Unit of contentA pageA passage that answers one question
How you measureRankings, clicksMentions, citations, accuracy of what AI says about you

AI assistants still rely heavily on search indexes and on well-structured websites. So AI SEO is not a replacement for SEO. It is SEO finished properly: the same site, made easier for a machine to quote.

An honest limit

Can anyone guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend you?

No. There is no ranking dial for ChatGPT, and anyone selling you one is guessing. AI answers change with the question, the user and the model version.

What this work does is improve the odds. It makes your business unambiguous, your facts machine-readable, and your answers the clearest available on the question. I show you what AI assistants say about you before and after, so you can judge the change yourself.

The work

What AI SEO work includes

I work on six things. Each one makes it easier for an AI assistant to identify your business and repeat accurate facts about it.

01

Entity clarity

An AI assistant has to know that "your studio", "your name" and "your address" are one business. I write one clear description of who you are, what you do and where, then use it consistently everywhere.

02

Structured data

Schema markup tells machines your business type, services, prices, location, opening hours and founder in a format they read reliably. I add it to every important page and test it in Google's Rich Results Test.

03

Answer-first content

Each service page opens with a direct answer of one or two sentences, then the details. FAQ sections cover the real questions your customers ask. These passages are what AI Overviews and ChatGPT tend to lift.

04

Consistent profiles and citations

AI assistants cross-check your website against your Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories and review sites. I align name, address, phone, services and description across all of them, so no source contradicts another.

05

llms.txt and crawl access

llms.txt is a short file that summarizes your site for AI tools. I write it, and I check that your robots.txt does not block the crawlers you want reading your site.

06

Measuring AI mentions

I ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode the questions your customers ask, before and after the work. You get a simple record: were you mentioned, were the facts right, and which sources were cited.

Fit

Who needs AI SEO?

AI SEO pays off when your customers research before they buy, and ask questions in full sentences. That is most service businesses with a considered purchase.

A good fit
  • Studios and clinics where clients research for weeks before booking, such as permanent makeup or tattoo
  • B2B services where a buyer asks an assistant for a shortlist of providers
  • Businesses that serve customers in more than one language
  • Anyone whose AI answers today are wrong: an old address, a closed branch, a service you no longer offer
Not worth it yet
  • A business with no website and no Google Business Profile; start with web design for service businesses
  • A business that needs enquiries this week; paid ads are faster

What AI assistants read about your business

AI assistants build their picture of you from several sources at once. The main ones are your website, your Google Business Profile, review platforms, business directories, LinkedIn and articles that mention you. If these sources disagree, the assistant either picks one at random or leaves you out. That is why consistency comes before anything clever.

FAQ

AI SEO questions

What is the difference between AI SEO, GEO and AEO?

They describe the same work. AI SEO is the umbrella term. GEO, generative engine optimization, focuses on tools that write answers, such as ChatGPT. AEO, answer engine optimization, focuses on structuring content so any engine can lift a direct answer.

Do I still need classic SEO if I do AI SEO?

Yes. AI assistants lean on search indexes and well-structured websites to find and check facts. A site that ranks poorly and says little is hard for an AI to recommend. AI SEO builds on classic SEO; it does not replace it.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. AI answers change with the question, the user and the model. AI SEO improves the odds that you are mentioned, and that what is said about you is accurate.

How do you measure AI SEO results?

I ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode 10-20 questions your customers really ask. I record whether you are mentioned, whether the facts are correct and which sources are cited. The same questions are repeated after the work and then monthly.

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of your website that summarizes who you are and links to your key pages. It gives AI tools a clean overview of your site. It is cheap to add and does no harm, but it is one small signal among many.

How long does AI SEO take to work?

Fixes to your profiles and structured data can show up in AI answers within a few weeks, as tools re-read the sources. Content changes usually take 1-3 months, the same as classic SEO.
