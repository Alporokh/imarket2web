The method

What do these projects have in common?

Every project followed the same order of operations. Demand research came first, trust questions went onto the page, and each language was built as its own market.

01

The page comes after the search

No sitemap gets drawn until I know what people actually type. The structure of the site mirrors the structure of the demand. That is why the service menus differ so much between these six.

02

Trust questions belong on the page

Does it hurt? How long does it last? Is my cargo insured? The questions people ask in DMs and phone calls get answered before the form, not after it.

03

Language is a market, not a toggle

Polish, English, Czech, Estonian and Russian versions are each built for how that audience searches. None of them is machine-translated over the top of the first.
