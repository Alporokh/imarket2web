What every project has in common

Different industries. The same order of operations.

01

The page comes after the search

No sitemap gets drawn until I know what people actually type. The structure of the site is the structure of the demand - which is why the service menus differ so much between these five.

02

Trust questions belong on the page

Does it hurt, how long does it last, what happens if something goes wrong, is my cargo insured. The questions asked in DMs and phone calls get answered before the form, not after it.

03

Language is a market, not a toggle

Polish, English, Czech, Estonian, Russian. Each language version is built for how that audience searches - not machine-translated over the top of the first one.
