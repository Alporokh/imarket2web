How the studio works

Why do I work alone?

I work alone on purpose: the person who audits your site is the person who writes it, sets up the tracking and answers the phone. Nothing gets lost in a handover, because there isn't one.

When you hire a studio, you usually meet the senior person once and then get handed to a junior. Here, you always talk to me.

It is also a real limit. I take on a small number of projects at a time, so I can give each one full attention.
